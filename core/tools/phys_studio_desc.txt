# phys_studio_desc.txt -- PHYSICS STUDIO labels, descriptions, units and natural bounds (w-physdesc). One line per distinct parameter name
# of the nine vehicle parameter files (the same name in several classes shares the line). Fields, separated by " | ":
#   name | label | unit | unit of a curve's x | bounds | description
# unit: the value's unit ("-" none, "ratio" a plain number; a curve: its y). The unit of a curve's end slopes is "unit/x unit".
# bounds: "-" the generic bounds (0 .. 5x the file's value; 5 % .. 5x for what must stay positive); otherwise a list of  lo=<n>x (a
#   multiple of the file's value) | lo=<n> | hi=<n>x | hi=<n> | toggle (a 0 / 1 switch); they apply to a value, or to a curve's y knots.
# The file's value always stays inside the bounds (the core widens them to hold it). Units are read from the original's code: the
# simulation works in feet, slugs (1 slug = 32.2 lb), seconds and pounds-force; engine speeds in the curves are rad/s, the registered
# limits rpm (the code divides by 9.55). "(unknown)" = the port could not read the unit / effect off the code.
# Vector values: X = sideways, Y = up, Z = forward of the vehicle. A value with three entries (tyre ones) is for the ground value
# 0 / 0.5 / 1 under the tyre (the TERRAIN page's tables decide what each ground gives; in between the game blends).
#
# ---- VehicleSim (the vehicle as a whole)
VehicleSim_Gravity | Gravity | ft/s2 | - | - | Gravity pulling the vehicle (X sideways, Y up, Z forward). Y is negative: lower it for floaty jumps, raise it for heavy ones.
VehicleSim_SpeedFilterTimeConstant | Speed smoothing time | s | - | - | How slowly the vehicle's speed reading is smoothed. The game uses it to tell when the vehicle has come to rest.
VehicleSim_GroundDragCurve | Ground drag vs speed | lb | ft/s | - | Air and rolling resistance on the ground, by forward speed: the faster you go the harder it pulls you back. Higher means a lower top speed.
VehicleSim_AirBorneDragCurve | Air drag vs speed | lb | ft/s | - | The same resistance while airborne, by forward speed. Higher slows the vehicle down in jumps.
VehicleSim_AirBorneDownForceCurve | Air downforce vs speed | lb | ft/s | - | Extra push down while airborne, by forward speed (four-wheel vehicles). Higher drops the nose of a jump faster at speed.
VehicleSim_MaxSteeringAngleCurve | Steering lock vs speed | rad | ft/s | lo=0 hi=1.5708 | The most the front wheel can turn, by forward speed. Low values at speed give stable high-speed riding. Capped at 90 degrees.
VehicleSim_SteeringTimeConstant | Steering response time | s | - | - | How long the steering takes to follow the stick. Lower is a sharper, twitchier front end.
VehicleSim_BikeLeanTimeConstant | Lean response time | s | - | - | How long the bike takes to lean to its target angle in a corner. Lower leans in faster.
VehicleSim_ThetaLeanMaxCurve | Lean angle vs speed | rad | ft/s | lo=0 hi=1.5708 | The lean angle the bike aims for at full steering, by forward speed. Higher leans over further. Capped at 90 degrees.
VehicleSim_UpVectorTimeConstant | Ground-up smoothing time | s | - | - | How slowly the bike's idea of the ground's up direction follows bumps. Higher gives a smoother, floatier ride over rough ground.
# ---- MassProps
MassProps_MainBodyCMOffset | Frame centre of mass offset | ft | - | - | Moves the frame's centre of mass (X sideways, Y up, Z forward). Higher or further back makes wheelies easier and the front lighter.
MassProps_MainBodyMass | Frame mass | slug | - | - | Mass of the bike or vehicle body (1 slug is 32.2 lb). Heavier is harder to throw around and slower to accelerate.
MassProps_RiderBodyMass | Rider mass | slug | - | - | Mass of the rider (1 slug is 32.2 lb). A heavier rider loads the suspension and shifts the balance.
MassProps_FrontTireMass | Front wheel mass | slug | - | - | Mass of the front wheel and tyre. Heavier needs more braking and steering effort and soaks up bumps differently.
MassProps_RearTireMass | Rear wheel mass | slug | - | - | Mass of the rear wheel and tyre. Heavier is harder to spin up.
MassProps_MainBodyInertia | Frame rotational inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | How hard the frame is to rotate about its sideways, up and forward axes (X pitch, Y yaw, Z roll). Higher is lazier in the air and in turns.
MassProps_RiderBodyInertia | Rider rotational inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | How hard the rider is to rotate (X, Y, Z axes). Affects how the rider's weight swings.
MassProps_FrontTireInertia | Front wheel inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | How hard the front wheel is to spin or tilt (the first value is its spin). Higher keeps the wheel turning and resists steering.
MassProps_RearTireInertia | Rear wheel inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | How hard the rear wheel is to spin or tilt (the first value is its spin). Higher is slower to rev up and slower to slow down.
MassProps_EngineInertia | Engine inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | How hard the engine is to rev up or slow down. Higher revs more slowly.
MassProps_DriveTrainInertia | Drivetrain inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | Spinning weight between the engine and the rear wheel. Higher is slower to accelerate.
MassProps_FrontAArmMass | Front arm mass | slug | - | - | Mass of the front suspension arm (four-wheel vehicles). It is 0 in the files and the port found nothing that reads it (no known effect).
MassProps_RearTrailingArmMass | Rear arm mass | slug | - | - | Mass of the rear suspension arm (four-wheel vehicles). It is 0 in the files and the port found nothing that reads it (no known effect).
MassProps_FrontAArmInertia | Front arm inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | Rotational inertia of the front suspension arm (four-wheel vehicles). It is 0 in the files and the port found nothing that reads it (no known effect).
MassProps_RearTrailingArmInertia | Rear arm inertia | slug ft2 | - | - | Rotational inertia of the rear suspension arm (four-wheel vehicles). It is 0 in the files and the port found nothing that reads it (no known effect).
